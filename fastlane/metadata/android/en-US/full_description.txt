NofAR is an offline-first Android app for hikers and travelers. Download map data for your area or a curated country pack, then point your phone at the horizon to see terrain-aware labels for peaks, cities, towns, and villages — filtered by real line-of-sight over the landscape.

No accounts. No backend. No tracking.

FEATURES
• Offline Explore — once map data is downloaded, Explore and Home work without network access
• Terrain-aware visibility — labels hide when ridges block the view, using live GLO-90 DEM raycasting
• Cell-based coverage — Simple mode downloads a 3×3 ring of 1° cells, or optional country packs (Israel, Switzerland, Greece)
• Home — manage downloaded map sets and enter Explore when coverage is ready
• Prepare — tap 1° cells on a map and download data (internet required; resumable)
• Explore — full-screen camera with compass-corrected AR labels
• Privacy by design — GPS-only location, no analytics or crash reporting that phones home
