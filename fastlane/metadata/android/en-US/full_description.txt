NofAR is an offline-first Android app for hikers and travelers. Download map data for a circular area, then point your phone at the horizon to see terrain-aware labels for peaks, cities, towns, and villages — filtered by real line-of-sight over the landscape.

No accounts. No backend. No tracking.

FEATURES
• Offline Explore — once a region is downloaded, Explore and Home work without network access
• Terrain-aware visibility — labels hide when ridges block the view, using live DEM raycasting
• Circular regions (5–20 km) — draw an area, download OpenStreetMap places/peaks and Copernicus elevation tiles
• Home — manage regions and enter Explore when you are inside a ready region
• Prepare — map widget to define a region and download data (internet required; resumable)
• Explore — full-screen camera with compass-corrected AR labels
• Privacy by design — GPS-only location, no analytics or crash reporting that phones home
